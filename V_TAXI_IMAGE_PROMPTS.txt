HOW TO USE THIS FILE
--------------------
1. Generate one asset at a time in ChatGPT Image Generation.
2. Copy the complete prompt under the required asset. Each prompt is standalone.
3. When a prompt asks for a transparent background, download as PNG and preserve transparency.
4. Generate important photography in at least two variations, then keep the one with the most natural vehicle geometry, faces, hands, road markings, and lighting.
5. Never ask the image model to add website headings, phone numbers, prices, buttons, city names, or Tamil writing. Add all copy in React/HTML so it stays accurate, responsive, accessible, and editable.
6. Use AVIF or WebP for photographs and backgrounds after generation. Keep transparent cutouts/icons as optimized WebP or PNG.
7. Use the suggested filename so implementation remains organized.
8. If the V TAXI logo must appear in an image, upload public/logo.png as Image 1 and explicitly identify it as the exact logo reference. Do not ask the model to redraw the logo from memory.

- Hero mobile: 4:5, ideally 1536 x 1920 or larger.
- Section/editorial images: 3:2 or 4:3.
- Service and destination card images: 4:3.
- Vehicle cutouts and icons: 1:1 with transparent background.
- Open Graph/social share cover: 1.91:1, ideally 1200 x 630.
- Do not bake rounded corners into an image; the website will apply them with CSS.
